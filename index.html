Professional Statement

I am a computational scientist; I provide meaningful insights and attain research goals by designing and implementing end-to-end bioinformatics pipelines. I have six years industry experience in agriculture and three years experience researching neurodegenerative diseases (particularly Parkinson's) and traumatic brain injury. Data modalities I have worked with include whole genome sequencing, RNA-seq, proteomics (targeted and untargeted), biomarker array assays, and clinical measures. I believe solutions to the complex health questions facing us today require multi-omic research techiniques, a hollistic approach to patient assessment, and personalized treatment and prevention plans.
