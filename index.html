Hello, I'm Jessie!
I'm very curious, and I love a challenge. This combination of traits can lead to trouble, but it also facilitates discovery, which is why I chose to become a research scientist.

In my current role as a bioinformatics analyst, I provide meaningful insights and attain research goals by designing and implementing end-to-end bioinformatics pipelines.

I have six years industry experience in agriculture and three years experience researching neurodegenerative diseases (particularly Parkinson's) and concussion.

Data modalities I have worked with include whole genome sequencing, RNA-seq, proteomics (targeted and untargeted), biomarker array assays, and clinical measures.

I believe solutions to the complex health questions facing us today require multi-omic research techiniques, a hollistic approach to patient assessment, and personalized treatment and prevention plans.
